Chinese Dramas Airing in December 2026: Titles, Synopsis, Cast & Release Dates

Published: November 24, 2026 · Updated: November 24, 2026

December 2026 brings a fresh wave of Chinese dramas across major streaming platforms such as Tencent Video, iQIYI, Youku, and Mango TV, blending costume fantasy, suspense thrillers, and contemporary romance.

Because C-drama release schedules are often announced close to premiere dates or rolled out as surprise drops, viewers typically see a mix of confirmed year-end launches and late-announced titles. Below is a helpful guide to key titles, release windows, cast lineups, and storylines to watch in December 2026.

Early December Picks: Costume Fantasy & Mystery

The Legend of Hou Ming (Expected Release: December 3, 2026 | Platform: Tencent Video) — A costume fantasy adventure following a spirited demon hunter and a cold-hearted celestial strategist who uncover an ancient seal threatening both the mortal and spirit realms. Cast: Hou Minghao, Tian Xiwei, and Wang Hongyi.

Endless Night

Endless Night (Expected Release: December 6, 2026 | Platform: iQIYI) — A suspense mystery thriller centered on a forensic analyst and a veteran detective reexamining a decade-old coastal disappearance after a series of copycat clues surfaces. Cast: Bai Yu, Wen Qi, and Liu Yijun.

♡ CewekPedia Tips

Always verify the final premiere date and VIP express release schedule on the official platform app (Tencent Video, iQIYI, Youku, or Mango TV), as C-dramas frequently shift dates or drop episodes without long advance notice.

Mid-December Releases: Modern Romance & Urban Drama

Mid-December leans into cozy winter romance and character-driven city stories suited for holiday binge-watching:

  • Winter Solstice Blossom (Expected Release: December 12, 2026 | Platform: Youku) — A warm modern romance about an architectural restorer and a quiet classical musician who cross paths in Harbin during winter and heal old family wounds together. Cast: Lin Yi, Zhang Ruonan, and Zhou Yiran.
  • Light Beyond the Harbor (Expected Release: December 16, 2026 | Platform: Mango TV) — A contemporary workplace and slice-of-life drama following a marine logistics planner and a documentary director navigating career reinvention and second chances. Cast: Tan Jianci, Sun Qian, and Xing Zhaolin.
  • Letters in the Snow (Expected Release: December 20, 2026 | Platform: iQIYI) — A romantic drama about two strangers exchanging handwritten letters through a small alpine post office who gradually discover their pasts are intertwined. Cast: Chen Zheyuan, Zhao Jinmai, and Deng Wei.

Late December & Year-End Watchlist Notes

Crimson Court Chronicles (Expected Release: December 26, 2026 | Platform: Tencent Video) — A palace intrigue and political drama focusing on a sharp-witted imperial archivist caught between rival factions during a succession crisis. Cast: Li Qin, Zhang Wanyi, and Ren Min. Keep an eye on platform year-end lineups as well, since several anticipated titles may still receive final air dates or be marked TBA pending official announcements.

Final Thoughts

From wintry romances to high-stakes mysteries and period adventures, December 2026 gives C-drama fans plenty of variety for year-end viewing. Bookmark the titles that fit your favorite genre and double-check platform announcements as premiere week approaches.
