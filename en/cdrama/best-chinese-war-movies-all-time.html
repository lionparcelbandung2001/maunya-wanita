8 Best Chinese War Movies of All Time: Stories, Cast, Release Dates & Awards

Published and updated: 7 November 2026

Chinese war cinema spans centuries of conflict and storytelling, from the Three Kingdoms era and the Qing dynasty to the Second Sino-Japanese War and the Korean War. Some titles dramatize documented events, while others blend historical figures, literary sources and cinematic invention.

This is an editorial selection rather than a definitive ranking. The films were chosen for their cultural impact, production scale, storytelling, popularity and award recognition. Release dates below refer to the films' mainland China theatrical releases.

Best Chinese War Movies to Watch

1. Red Cliff (2008)

China release date: 10 Juli 2008   |   Director: John Woo

Main cast: Tony Leung Chiu-wai, Takeshi Kaneshiro, Zhang Fengyi, Chang Chen, Zhao Wei, Hu Jun

Synopsis: In the late Han dynasty, Chancellor Cao Cao marches a vast army south to defeat rival forces. Outnumbered, the forces of Sun Quan and Liu Bei form an alliance. Zhuge Liang and Zhou Yu develop a strategy built around terrain, weather and the weaknesses of Cao Cao's fleet. The film follows the events leading to the famous Battle of Red Cliffs.

Awards and recognition: Received multiple international nominations, including Golden Horse and Saturn Award nominations, with recognition in areas such as music and costume design.

Note: The story is a loose adaptation of historical events and the novel Romance of the Three Kingdoms. The Asian release was split into two parts.

2. The Warlords (2007)

China release date: 12 Desember 2007   |   Director: Peter Chan

Main cast: Jet Li, Andy Lau, Takeshi Kaneshiro, Xu Jinglei

Synopsis: Set during the Taiping Rebellion in the 19th century, the film follows three men who become sworn brothers amid chaos. They build a military force and win battles, but political ambition, loyalty, love and betrayal gradually test their bond. The story explores the moral and personal costs of war, not just battlefield victories.

Awards and recognition: Won the 2008 Hong Kong Film Award for Best Film and Best Actor (Jet Li), as well as the 2008 Golden Horse Award for Best Feature Film and Best Director.

Note: The film draws on historical episodes and late-Qing legends, with dramatic invention.

3. Assembly (2007)

China release date: 20 Desember 2007   |   Director: Feng Xiaogang

Main cast: Zhang Hanyu, Deng Chao, Yuan Wenkang, Tang Yan, Wang Baoqiang, Liao Fan, Hu Jun

Synopsis: Captain Gu Zidi leads a company during the Chinese Civil War. After a devastating battle, he becomes determined to secure recognition for his missing comrades. Years later, he continues searching for the truth and for the honour owed to the soldiers who died. Intense combat scenes are paired with a moving story about memory and sacrifice.

Awards and recognition: Zhang Hanyu won the 2008 Golden Horse Award for Best Leading Actor. The film won the 2009 Golden Rooster Award for Best Film and the Golden Horse Award for Best Adapted Screenplay.

Note: A war film focused on ordinary soldiers and the long aftermath of battle.

4. City of Life and Death (Nanjing! Nanjing!) (2009)

China release date: 22 April 2009   |   Director: Lu Chuan

Main cast: Liu Ye, Gao Yuanyuan, Fan Wei, Hideo Nakaizumi, Qin Lan, Jiang Yiyan

Synopsis: Set in Nanjing in December 1937, the film portrays the city's fall during the Second Sino-Japanese War through the experiences of soldiers, civilians and people in the International Safety Zone. It depicts a devastated city, desperate efforts to survive and the human tragedy of occupation. This is a serious and emotionally difficult film.

Awards and recognition: Lu Chuan won Achievement in Directing, and the film received Achievement in Cinematography at the 2009 Asia Pacific Screen Awards. It also won the Golden Seashell at the 2009 San Sebastián International Film Festival.

Note: It depicts real historical violence. Viewers should expect scenes of war and violence against civilians.

5. The Eight Hundred (2020)

China release date: 21 Agustus 2020   |   Director: Guan Hu

Main cast: Huang Zhizhong, Oho Ou, Wang Qianyuan, Jiang Wu, Zhang Yi, Du Chun, Vision Wei, Li Chen

Synopsis: In 1937, a group of Chinese soldiers holds out inside Sihang Warehouse in Shanghai as Japanese forces surround the area. Isolated in the building, the defenders try to repel attacks while maintaining their resolve in front of the enemy and onlookers across the river. The film emphasizes the scale of battle, courage and the cost of holding a position.

Awards and recognition: Won the 2021 Golden Reel Award for sound editing in a foreign-language feature and the 2021 Asian Film Award for Best Visual Effects. Guan Hu also won Best Director at the 2020 Macau International Movie Festival.

Note: Inspired by the 1937 defence of Sihang Warehouse during the Battle of Shanghai. The popular figure of '800' differs from some historical counts.

6. The Sacrifice (2020)

China release date: 23 Oktober 2020   |   Director: Guan Hu, Frant Gwo, Lu Yang

Main cast: Zhang Yi, Wu Jing, Li Jiuxiao, Vision Wei, Deng Chao

Synopsis: Set during the Korean War in 1953, the film follows Chinese forces trying to maintain a crossing over the Kumgang River amid air and artillery attacks. Told through several perspectives, it shows soldiers, engineers and commanders facing the same dangerous mission. Its repeated structure revisits events from different points of view.

Awards and recognition: Received several festival awards and nominations, including the 2020 Golden Lotus Awards for Best Actor (Zhang Yi) and Best Writing (Guan Hu) at the Macau International Movie Festival.

Note: A dramatization of the Korean War presented from a Chinese production perspective.

7. The Battle at Lake Changjin (2021)

China release date: 30 September 2021   |   Director: Chen Kaige, Tsui Hark, Dante Lam

Main cast: Wu Jing, Jackson Yee, Duan Yihong, Zhu Yawen, Li Chen, Hu Jun

Synopsis: Set during the 1950 Battle of Chosin Reservoir in the Korean War, the film follows brothers Wu Qianli and Wu Wanli as they join troops facing extreme winter conditions. They confront major battles, shortages and difficult decisions in a snow-covered landscape. The film is known for its enormous production scale and epic battle sequences.

Awards and recognition: Won Outstanding Film and Outstanding Director at the 2022 Huabiao Film Awards. It also won Best Film at the 2021 Changchun Film Festival.

Note: The film presents the Korean War from a Chinese perspective; historical interpretations may differ among international sources.

8. The Battle at Lake Changjin II: Water Gate Bridge (2022)

China release date: 1 Februari 2022   |   Director: Tsui Hark, Chen Kaige, Dante Lam

Main cast: Wu Jing, Jackson Yee, Duan Yihong, Zhu Yawen, Li Chen, Hu Jun

Synopsis: This sequel continues the story after the fighting around Lake Changjin. The Seventh Company is assigned to attack and destroy the Shuimen Bridge, a key route for enemy forces. In extreme weather and with their strength fading, the soldiers must complete the mission through a series of dangerous assaults.

Awards and recognition: A major commercial title and part of a popular Chinese war-film series. No major award win is listed here without stronger verification.

Note: A direct continuation of The Battle at Lake Changjin (2021), rather than a fully standalone story.

Conclusion

The best Chinese war movies offer very different experiences. Red Cliff is ideal for viewers drawn to ancient military strategy, while Assembly and The Warlords focus on loyalty and the human cost of conflict. City of Life and Death is a sobering historical drama; The Eight Hundred and The Battle at Lake Changjin deliver large-scale modern battle sequences. Choose according to your interests, and remember that historical films may dramatize real events.

Editorial note: the awards listed are selected highlights, not exhaustive award records. International release dates may differ from mainland China's dates. Historical films can also reflect a particular national or cinematic interpretation of past events.
