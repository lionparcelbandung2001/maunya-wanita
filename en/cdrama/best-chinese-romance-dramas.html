10 Best Chinese Romance Dramas of All Time, with Plot Summaries and Release Dates

Published: November 5, 2026 · Updated: November 5, 2026

Chinese dramas, also known as C-dramas, have become increasingly popular among fans of romantic television series around the world. Their stories cover a wide range of themes, from first love in high school and sweet university relationships to workplace romance and fantasy love stories set in ancient kingdoms.

Many Chinese romance dramas have captured viewers' hearts, making them smile, cry, and think about the characters long after the final episode. Some titles are still widely recommended years after their original release.

If you are looking for a romantic series to enjoy during your free time, here are ten Chinese dramas worth adding to your watchlist. This is an editorial selection by CewekPedia based on popularity, storytelling, and romantic appeal, rather than an official ranking.

10 Best Chinese Romance Dramas to Add to Your Watchlist

1. Love O2O (2016)

Original release date: August 22, 2016

Main cast: Yang Yang, Zheng Shuang

Genres: Romance, Comedy, School, Gaming

Episodes: 30

Love O2O follows Bei Weiwei, a smart university student who is also known as a talented female player in an online game. Her gaming skills eventually bring her into contact with Xiao Nai, a popular university student who is also an exceptional player.

The two first get to know each other through the virtual gaming world. However, their connection gradually develops into a real-life romance. Although they face several challenges, communication and trust help make their relationship stronger.

This drama is a great choice for viewers who enjoy lighthearted campus romance, sweet interactions, and a story without too much heavy conflict.

2. Eternal Love (2017)

Original release date: January 30, 2017

Main cast: Yang Mi, Mark Chao

Genres: Romance, Fantasy, Historical, Xianxia

Episodes: 58

Eternal Love, also known as Ten Miles of Peach Blossoms, tells the love story of Bai Qian, a goddess from the Fox Tribe, and Ye Hua, the Crown Prince of the Heavenly Realm.

Their relationship is far from easy. They must face sacrifice, misunderstandings, memory loss, and numerous trials involving both the immortal and human worlds. Although fate repeatedly separates them, their love continues to find a way back.

This drama is an excellent choice for fans of fantasy romance, beautiful ancient settings, and emotionally powerful storytelling.

3. A Love So Beautiful (2017)

Original release date: November 9, 2017

Main cast: Hu Yitian, Shen Yue

Genres: Romance, Comedy, Youth, School

Episodes: 23 episodes and 1 special episode

A Love So Beautiful follows Chen Xiaoxi, a cheerful schoolgirl who has a crush on her childhood friend and next-door neighbor, Jiang Chen. Unlike the expressive Xiaoxi, Jiang Chen is quiet and often appears cold.

From their high school days into adulthood, Xiaoxi continues to show her feelings for him. Their relationship is filled with funny moments, jealousy, friendship, and the ups and downs of first love.

This drama is perfect for viewers who enjoy nostalgic school romance and sweet coming-of-age stories.

4. Put Your Head on My Shoulder (2019)

Original release date: April 10, 2019

Main cast: Xing Fei, Lin Yi

Genres: Romance, Comedy, Youth, Campus

Episodes: 24

Si Tu Mo is a university student nearing graduation who is still unsure about her future. Her life takes an unexpected turn when she meets Gu Weiyi, a brilliant physics student with a rather awkward and serious personality.

Due to an unexpected situation, the two eventually end up living together. What begins as an awkward arrangement slowly turns into a sweet romantic relationship.

Their everyday interactions create plenty of adorable moments. This series is a lovely pick for anyone who enjoys light romance and charming couple dynamics.

5. You Are My Glory (2021)

Original release date: July 26, 2021

Main cast: Yang Yang, Dilraba Dilmurat

Genres: Romance, Modern, Drama

Episodes: 32

Qiao Jingjing is a famous actress who wants to improve her gaming skills for a work-related project. During this process, she reconnects with Yu Tu, a former schoolmate she once admired.

Yu Tu now works as an aerospace engineer and lives a very different life from Jingjing's entertainment career. Their reunion brings old feelings back to the surface.

As they navigate demanding careers and different lifestyles, the two gradually build a closer relationship. The drama is appealing because it portrays mature romance filled with warmth, encouragement, and mutual support.

6. Love Between Fairy and Devil (2022)

Original release date: August 7, 2022

Main cast: Esther Yu, Dylan Wang

Genres: Romance, Fantasy, Historical, Xianxia

Episodes: 36

This drama follows Xiao Lanhua, a small orchid spirit who accidentally frees Dongfang Qingcang, a feared and powerful ruler of the demon realm.

The two have completely different personalities and come from opposite worlds. However, a mysterious magical connection binds them together and forces them to experience a series of unexpected events.

As the story unfolds, their relationship gradually changes from conflict into love. Dongfang Qingcang, who is known for his cold personality, begins to reveal a softer side when he is with Xiao Lanhua.

This series is a great choice for fans of fantasy romance, stunning visuals, major conflicts, and characters with emotional depth.

7. Meet Yourself (2023)

Original release date: January 3, 2023

Main cast: Liu Yifei, Li Xian

Genres: Romance, Life, Slice of Life

Episodes: 40

Xu Hongdou is a woman going through a difficult period after losing a close friend. Looking for peace and time to heal, she decides to leave her busy city life behind and travel to a village in Dali, Yunnan.

There, she meets Xie Zhiyao, a man who is working to develop and improve his hometown. Their meeting marks the beginning of a new journey filled with friendship, personal healing, and romance.

Unlike romantic dramas filled with dramatic twists, Meet Yourself offers a peaceful atmosphere, beautiful scenery, and a love story that develops naturally over time.

8. When I Fly Towards You (2023)

Original release date: June 13, 2023

Main cast: Zhang Miaoyi, Zhou Yiran

Genres: Romance, Youth, School, Comedy

Episodes: 24

Su Zaizai is a cheerful schoolgirl who becomes interested in Zhang Lurang the moment she first meets him at school. Unlike Zaizai, who is full of energy, Lurang is quiet and difficult to approach.

Zaizai's presence gradually changes Lurang's life. Along with their friends, the two experience school life, friendship, personal ambitions, and the excitement of first love.

The drama features plenty of simple but heartwarming romantic moments. It is a wonderful choice for viewers who enjoy wholesome and cheerful teenage romance.

9. Hidden Love (2023)

Original release date: June 20, 2023

Main cast: Zhao Lusi, Chen Zheyuan

Genres: Romance, Youth, Modern

Episodes: 25

Hidden Love tells the story of Sang Zhi, a young girl who secretly develops feelings for Duan Jiaxu, her older brother's university friend. Her feelings begin when Duan Jiaxu visits her family home several times.

When she is younger, Sang Zhi can only keep her feelings to herself. Years later, they meet again when she has grown into an adult.

Their connection gradually develops into a more serious romantic relationship. Duan Jiaxu begins to understand Sang Zhi's feelings, while Sang Zhi wants to show him how much she has grown.

Hidden Love is a wonderful recommendation for viewers who enjoy gentle romance, thoughtful gestures, and a warm love story.

10. Amidst a Snowstorm of Love (2024)

Original release date: February 2, 2024

Main cast: Wu Lei, Zhao Jinmai

Genres: Romance, Sports, Modern

Episodes: 30

Lin Yiyang is a former professional snooker player who left the sport after a difficult incident during a competition. His life takes an unexpected turn when he meets Yin Guo, a billiards player visiting Finland.

Their meeting during a snowstorm becomes the beginning of a growing connection. Yin Guo gradually learns about Lin Yiyang's past and understands the reasons behind the major decision he once made.

The two support each other in both their personal lives and sporting careers. Their romance feels warm and comforting, with a beautiful winter atmosphere adding to the story.

Which Chinese Romance Drama Should You Watch First?

Every drama on this list has something special to offer. If you enjoy stories about first love, A Love So Beautiful and When I Fly Towards You are excellent choices. Both feature nostalgic school settings and memorable youthful experiences.

For viewers who prefer mature romantic relationships, You Are My Glory and Meet Yourself offer more grown-up love stories. Hidden Love is a lovely option if you enjoy watching a relationship develop from a quiet crush into something deeper.

If fantasy is more your style, Eternal Love and Love Between Fairy and Devil offer romantic stories involving destiny, sacrifice, and magical ancient kingdoms.

Why Are Chinese Romance Dramas So Popular?

There are several reasons why Chinese romantic dramas have attracted viewers from different parts of the world.

  • Variety of stories: From school and university life to workplace relationships and fantasy worlds, there is something for every viewer.
  • Interesting characters: Many dramas feature characters who grow and change throughout the story.
  • Beautiful visuals: Cinematography, costumes, filming locations, and scenery often add to the viewing experience.
  • Emotional storytelling: Themes of love, family, friendship, and personal struggles help viewers connect with the characters.
  • Memorable soundtracks: Music often makes romantic and emotional scenes even more powerful.

Where Can You Watch Chinese Dramas?

Some Chinese dramas are available on official streaming platforms such as Netflix, Viu, iQIYI, WeTV, Youku, and Viki. However, the availability of individual titles, subtitle languages, and subscription options may vary depending on your country and the time you check.

It is always a good idea to check each platform's official catalogue before watching. This helps you find legitimate versions with good video quality and the subtitles you need.

Final Thoughts

Chinese romance dramas offer a wide variety of stories that can make your free time more enjoyable. From simple school romances to emotional love stories filled with sacrifice in fantasy worlds, every title has its own unique appeal.

The ten dramas featured above are a great starting point if you are looking for memorable Chinese romantic series. You do not have to follow the list in order. Simply choose the story that best matches your interests and mood.
