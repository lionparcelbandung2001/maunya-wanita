15 Best Korean Royal and Historical Dramas of All Time

Published: November 13, 2026 · Updated: November 13, 2026

Korean historical dramas have a special charm that keeps viewers coming back. From magnificent royal palaces and powerful kings to forbidden love, political conspiracies, family rivalries, and unforgettable battles, these stories offer much more than beautiful costumes and traditional settings.

Known as sageuk, Korean historical dramas explore different periods of Korean history, including the Three Kingdoms era, Goryeo, and Joseon. Some productions follow historical events, while others combine royal settings with romance, fantasy, comedy, or supernatural elements.

If you are looking for a memorable historical K-drama to watch, here are 15 outstanding titles worth adding to your list. This is an editorial selection based on storytelling, genre influence, popularity, and the variety of experiences each series offers—not an official ranking.

1. Jewel in the Palace (2003–2004)

A timeless classic about ambition and determination

Jewel in the Palace, also known as Dae Jang Geum, is one of the most iconic Korean historical dramas ever made. The series follows Seo Jang-geum, an orphaned girl who works her way through the royal kitchen before pursuing a remarkable path in traditional medicine.

Her journey is filled with rivalry, difficult challenges, palace politics, and personal sacrifice. What makes the drama special is its focus on a determined woman who refuses to give up despite the limitations placed on her.

The series is an excellent choice for viewers who enjoy traditional palace life, royal court customs, cooking, medicine, and inspiring female characters.

2. Six Flying Dragons (2015–2016)

A grand political story about the birth of a dynasty

Six Flying Dragons tells the story of the turbulent period surrounding the fall of Goryeo and the establishment of the Joseon Dynasty. It follows several influential characters whose ambitions, beliefs, and political decisions shape the future of the country.

The drama features power struggles, military conflicts, strategic alliances, and complicated relationships between political leaders. It is particularly appealing to viewers who enjoy detailed historical storytelling and intelligent political strategies.

With its large cast and long-form narrative, this series offers an immersive look at the struggles behind the creation of a new kingdom.

3. Mr. Sunshine (2018)

A cinematic historical romance with a powerful story

Mr. Sunshine takes place in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, during a period of major political and social change in Korea. The story follows Eugene Choi, a man born into a difficult life who returns to Korea as an American military officer.

There, he meets Go Ae-shin, a noblewoman secretly involved in the struggle to protect her homeland. Their relationship develops against a backdrop of political tension, foreign influence, and growing resistance.

The drama is widely admired for its cinematography, emotional performances, memorable characters, and dramatic historical atmosphere.

4. Moon Lovers: Scarlet Heart Ryeo (2016)

Time travel, royal rivalry, and heartbreaking romance

Moon Lovers: Scarlet Heart Ryeo follows a modern woman named Go Ha-jin who travels back in time to the Goryeo Dynasty during a solar eclipse. She awakens in the body of Hae-soo and becomes involved with several royal princes.

As the princes compete for power and the throne, Hae-soo finds herself caught between political conflicts and complicated feelings. The series combines historical palace drama with fantasy and romance.

It is especially memorable for its emotional storyline, royal family conflicts, and the difficult choices faced by its characters.

5. The Red Sleeve (2021–2022)

A moving romance between a king and a palace woman

The Red Sleeve tells the story of Crown Prince Yi San, who later becomes King Jeongjo, and Seong Deok-im, a palace maid who values her freedom and personal choices.

Their relationship grows in a world where royal responsibilities and palace traditions often conflict with personal happiness. The drama explores the emotional cost of love when one person belongs to the royal family and the other wants to preserve her independence.

For viewers who enjoy realistic palace settings, restrained romance, and emotional storytelling, this is an excellent choice.

6. Kingdom (2019–2020)

Royal politics meets a terrifying zombie outbreak

Kingdom offers a different experience from traditional historical dramas. Set during Korea's Joseon period, the story follows Crown Prince Lee Chang as he investigates a mysterious illness spreading throughout the kingdom.

The disease turns the dead into dangerous creatures, creating a terrifying crisis. At the same time, the crown prince must face political conspiracies and powerful figures who threaten his position.

With its combination of historical settings, horror, action, and palace intrigue, Kingdom became one of the most distinctive Korean period dramas for international audiences.

♡ CewekPedia Tips

Not every sageuk has the same atmosphere. If you prefer traditional royal politics, try Six Flying Dragons or Jewel in the Palace. For romance, choose The Red Sleeve or Moon Lovers. If you enjoy horror and action, Kingdom is a great option.

7. Dong Yi (2010)

The journey of a woman who rises from humble beginnings

Dong Yi follows a young woman from a low social background who enters the royal palace and gradually becomes an important figure in the royal court.

The story focuses on her intelligence, determination, and ability to survive in a world filled with political competition and palace rivalries. Her journey also connects her to King Sukjong and the future royal family.

This drama is a good choice for viewers who enjoy long historical series with strong female characters and detailed palace politics.

8. Jumong (2006–2007)

An epic story of a legendary kingdom founder

Jumong is a historical epic inspired by the legendary founder of Goguryeo, one of the ancient Korean kingdoms.

The story follows Jumong as he grows from a young prince into a determined leader. Along the way, he faces political enemies, military challenges, personal sacrifices, and struggles over his identity and destiny.

With its large-scale battles, royal conflicts, and themes of leadership, Jumong is a strong recommendation for viewers who enjoy epic historical adventures.

9. The Moon Embracing the Sun (2012)

A royal romance surrounded by secrets and destiny

The Moon Embracing the Sun tells the story of a young crown prince who falls in love with a noblewoman. Their relationship is disrupted by palace conspiracies and events that separate them for years.

When they meet again as adults, the woman has become a royal shaman, while the prince has ascended to the throne. Their feelings remain, but the secrets surrounding their past threaten their future.

The series combines romance, royal mystery, political intrigue, and supernatural elements in a memorable historical setting.

10. Mr. Queen (2020–2021)

A hilarious body-swap story inside the royal palace

Mr. Queen brings a fresh and humorous twist to the historical genre. The story begins when the soul of a modern male chef unexpectedly ends up inside the body of Queen Kim So-yong during the Joseon Dynasty.

The unexpected situation creates plenty of comedy as the queen struggles to adapt to palace traditions, royal expectations, and the complicated relationship with King Cheoljong.

Although it features plenty of humor, the drama also includes political schemes, royal secrets, and character development. It is ideal for viewers who want something lighter than a traditional historical melodrama.

11. The Crowned Clown (2019)

A dangerous royal identity switch

The Crowned Clown follows a king who is surrounded by enemies and political threats. To protect himself, a clown who looks almost identical to the king is brought into the palace to take his place.

The clown suddenly finds himself living as a ruler, facing royal responsibilities and dangerous political decisions. As he becomes more involved in palace life, the difference between pretending to be a king and becoming a true leader becomes increasingly complicated.

This drama is particularly interesting for viewers who enjoy psychological tension, royal conspiracies, and strong acting performances.

12. Rookie Historian Goo Hae-ryung (2019)

A woman challenges the rules of the royal court

Rookie Historian Goo Hae-ryung follows Goo Hae-ryung, a young woman who wants to become a palace historian at a time when women are rarely allowed to hold such positions.

Her work brings her into contact with Prince Yi Rim, a royal figure with his own secrets and ambitions. Together, they navigate social expectations, palace traditions, and personal challenges.

The series combines romance, comedy, and historical themes while highlighting the importance of education, independence, and women's opportunities.

13. Hwarang: The Poet Warrior Youth (2016–2017)

Friendship, loyalty, and young warriors in ancient Silla

Hwarang is set during the Silla Kingdom and follows a group of young men who become members of an elite group known as the Hwarang.

The story explores friendship, loyalty, romance, identity, and the responsibilities of young warriors. It also includes royal secrets and political tensions surrounding the kingdom.

With its youthful cast and lighter tone, Hwarang is a good option for viewers who enjoy historical settings mixed with friendship and romance.

14. Alchemy of Souls (2022–2023)

Magic, royal secrets, and a fictional historical world

Alchemy of Souls is a fantasy historical drama set in the fictional country of Daeho. The story follows powerful mages and characters whose lives are changed by a forbidden spell that allows souls to move between bodies.

At the center of the story are Jang Uk and Naksu, whose relationship develops amid magical conflicts, hidden identities, and dangerous secrets.

Although it is not a conventional historical drama based on a specific Korean dynasty, its palace atmosphere, costumes, political conflicts, and fantasy world make it a memorable choice for fans of historical fantasy.

15. Bon Appétit, Your Majesty (2025)

A modern chef travels into the world of a Joseon king

Bon Appétit, Your Majesty brings time travel, food, romance, and royal politics together in a modern historical fantasy story.

The series follows a talented modern chef who unexpectedly travels back to the Joseon era. There, she encounters a powerful king known for his demanding personality and refined taste in food.

Her cooking skills become an important part of her survival as she navigates the unfamiliar world of the royal palace. The drama offers a lighter approach to historical storytelling, with a combination of culinary creativity, romance, and palace intrigue.

How to Choose the Right Korean Historical Drama

With so many different stories available, choosing a historical K-drama can feel overwhelming. The best starting point depends on the kind of experience you enjoy.

  • For classic palace life: Jewel in the Palace and Dong Yi.
  • For political intrigue: Six Flying Dragons and The Crowned Clown.
  • For emotional romance: The Red Sleeve and Moon Lovers: Scarlet Heart Ryeo.
  • For fantasy and magic: Alchemy of Souls and The Moon Embracing the Sun.
  • For action and horror: Kingdom and Jumong.
  • For comedy: Mr. Queen and Bon Appétit, Your Majesty.
  • For strong female characters: Jewel in the Palace and Rookie Historian Goo Hae-ryung.

You may also want to consider the length of each series. Some classic sageuk dramas have many episodes and take time to develop their stories, while newer productions often have shorter seasons and faster pacing.

Why Korean Historical Dramas Remain Popular

One reason sageuk dramas remain appealing is their ability to combine history with universal human emotions. Stories about ambition, family, love, loyalty, betrayal, and the search for freedom can connect with viewers regardless of their cultural background.

The genre has also evolved. Traditional royal dramas now share the spotlight with fantasy adventures, supernatural mysteries, comedy, romance, and action. This variety allows new audiences to discover historical Korean storytelling in different ways.

Whether you prefer a realistic palace story or an imaginative fantasy set in a royal world, there is likely a Korean historical drama that matches your taste.

Final Thoughts

The best Korean royal and historical dramas are not simply stories about kings, queens, and palaces. They explore the people behind the titles—their dreams, sacrifices, relationships, and struggles to shape their own destinies.

From the timeless journey of Jewel in the Palace to the political scale of Six Flying Dragons, the emotional romance of The Red Sleeve, and the fantasy world of Alchemy of Souls, every series offers something different.

If you are new to sageuk, start with the story that interests you most. You might discover a new favorite genre along the way.
