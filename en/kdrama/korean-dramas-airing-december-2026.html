Korean Dramas Airing in December 2026: Titles, Synopsis, Cast & Release Dates

Published: November 24, 2026 · Updated: November 24, 2026

December 2026 closes out the K-drama calendar with a stacked lineup of premieres and returning seasons. Viewers can expect a mix of sports fantasy, historical action, sci-fi crime thrillers, showbiz epics, and romance.

Several major A-list stars are headlining year-end releases across broadcast networks and streaming platforms, including Kim Woo-bin, Song Hye-kyo, Gong Yoo, Lee Je-hoon, Kim Hye-soo, Bae Doona, and Ahn Bo-hyun. Below is a roundup of notable Korean dramas scheduled to air in December 2026 along with their release dates, cast members, and synopses.

Early December Releases: Gift and Sacred Jewel

Gift (Release Date: December 5, 2026 | Network/Platform: tvN / Netflix) — A 12-episode sports fantasy drama adapted from a webtoon. Following an accident, former professional pitching coach Jung Min-yong gains a special ability to spot hidden baseball talent and sets out to turn around a bottom-ranked high school team. Cast: Kim Woo-bin, Seo Eun-soo, Seo Hyun-woo, and Lim Su-bin.

Sacred Jewel (The Bead of God)

Sacred Jewel (Release Date: December 5, 2026 | Network/Platform: JTBC / Prime Video) — Set in 1258 during the Goryeo Dynasty amid prolonged conflict with Mongol forces, this historical action drama follows a royal guard mission to secure sacred relics alongside a princess working to rescue them. Cast: Ahn Bo-hyun, Lee Sung-min, Claudia Kim, Ha Yoon-kyung, and Yoon Kyun-sang.

Mid-December Highlights: The Second Signal, Tantara, and Your Ground

Mid-December brings high-profile returns and star-studded new titles across thriller, period music drama, and romance:

  • The Second Signal (Release Date: December 7, 2026 | Network: tvN) — Sequel to the 2016 hit crime thriller, where investigators in the present and a detective in the past connect through a mysterious walkie-talkie to solve long-unsolved cold cases. Cast: Lee Je-hoon, Kim Hye-soo, and Cho Jin-woong.
  • Tantara (Release Date: December 11, 2026 | Platform: Netflix) — A 22-episode period drama spanning the 1960s to 1980s about childhood friends navigating the competitive South Korean music and entertainment industry. Cast: Song Hye-kyo, Gong Yoo, Kim Seol-hyun, and Cha Seung-won.
  • Your Ground (Release Date: December 11, 2026 | Network: MBC) — A youth, sports, and romance series centered on a talented baseball player who pauses his career after a setback and finds a new path after meeting a lawyer-turned-sports agent. Cast: Han Hyo-joo, Gong Myung, Kim Shin-rok, and Sung Joon.

Late December & Additional Watchlist Picks

Family Matters Season 2 (Release Date: December 18, 2026 | Platform: Coupang Play) — Continues the action-thriller fantasy story of Han Young-soo and her unconventional family with special abilities as they relocate to a new city and face dangerous adversaries tied to their past. Cast: Bae Doona, Baek Yoon-sik, Park Byung-eun, Lomon, and Lee Soo-hyun. Other titles slated for December 2026 include Portraits of Delusion on Disney+ starring Kim Seon-ho and Bae Suzy (exact premiere date to be confirmed), as well as ongoing weekly episodes of The Substitute.

Final Thoughts

Whether you prefer gritty investigative mysteries, historical epics, nostalgic showbiz sagas, or underdog sports stories, December 2026 offers plenty of options to wrap up the year. Pick a couple of premieres that match your favorite mood and add them to your watchlist.
