15 Best Korean Comedy Dramas of All Time

Published: November 13, 2026 · Updated: November 13, 2026

Sometimes, the best way to end a long day is to watch a Korean drama that makes you laugh. Whether it is a ridiculous misunderstanding, an awkward romantic moment, a chaotic office, or a group of friends getting into trouble, Korean comedy dramas have a special way of turning ordinary situations into unforgettable kdrama.

Korean comedy is not limited to romantic stories. Some series focus on family life, friendship, workplace struggles, school memories, or pure slapstick humor. Others combine comedy with romance, action, fantasy, and even crime.

If you are searching for something entertaining, here are 15 memorable Korean comedy dramas worth watching. This is an editorial selection that considers humor, storytelling, memorable characters, and lasting audience appeal rather than an official ranking.

1. Welcome to Waikiki (2018–2019)

A hilarious guesthouse full of unexpected disasters

Welcome to Waikiki is one of the first titles that comes to mind when discussing laugh-out-loud Korean dramas. The story follows three young men who run a guesthouse called Waikiki while struggling to achieve their dreams.

Their everyday lives become increasingly chaotic when unexpected guests, financial problems, romantic misunderstandings, and ridiculous situations take over the guesthouse.

The series is known for its exaggerated comedy, physical humor, awkward moments, and lovable characters. It is an excellent choice for viewers who want something genuinely funny without having to follow a complicated storyline.

2. Reply 1988 (2015–2016)

A heartwarming comedy about friendship, family, and growing up

Reply 1988 is much more than a nostalgic television drama. Set in a neighborhood in Seoul during the late 1980s, it follows five childhood friends and their families as they experience the ups and downs of everyday life.

The humor comes naturally from family arguments, funny neighborhood interactions, teenage awkwardness, and the unique personalities of the characters. At the same time, the series explores friendship, first love, family sacrifices, and the meaning of home.

For viewers who enjoy comedy with emotional depth, Reply 1988 is a wonderful choice. It can make you laugh in one scene and feel deeply emotional in the next.

3. Mr. Queen (2020–2021)

A modern chef trapped inside a queen's body

Mr. Queen delivers a wonderfully unusual comedy concept. A modern-day male chef suddenly finds his soul inside the body of Queen Kim So-yong during the Joseon Dynasty.

The queen's unexpected personality creates chaos inside the royal palace. From unusual behavior and strange cooking habits to hilarious interactions with King Cheoljong, almost every situation becomes an opportunity for comedy.

Beyond the humor, the drama also features palace politics, royal secrets, and an evolving relationship between the central characters. It is especially enjoyable for viewers who like historical settings with a modern comedic twist.

4. Strong Woman Do Bong Soon (2017)

A tiny woman with extraordinary strength

Strong Woman Do Bong Soon follows Do Bong-soon, a young woman born with incredible physical strength. Although she looks small and gentle, her unusual ability makes her capable of doing things that ordinary people cannot.

Her life becomes more complicated when she starts working as a bodyguard for a wealthy and playful company CEO. Their relationship develops through funny conversations, romantic moments, and unexpected adventures.

The series mixes romantic comedy, action, and a crime storyline. Its playful characters and humorous situations make it a popular choice for viewers who enjoy light kdrama.

5. Weightlifting Fairy Kim Bok-joo (2016–2017)

Young love, sports, friendship, and everyday comedy

Weightlifting Fairy Kim Bok-joo follows Kim Bok-joo, a university student and aspiring weightlifter who is learning to balance her athletic ambitions with friendship and romance.

The story explores campus life, training routines, personal insecurities, and the funny situations that happen when young people experience their first serious relationships.

The chemistry between the main characters gives the series much of its charm. It is a warm and cheerful drama for anyone who enjoys youthful romance mixed with relatable comedy.

6. What's Wrong with Secretary Kim (2018)

A self-absorbed boss and his indispensable secretary

What's Wrong with Secretary Kim centers on Lee Young-joon, a wealthy and extremely confident vice chairman who believes he is almost perfect.

His organized secretary, Kim Mi-so, suddenly announces that she wants to resign after working for him for years. Her decision forces Young-joon to reconsider his life and discover why he cannot imagine working without her.

The drama combines workplace humor, romantic misunderstandings, charming chemistry, and a personal mystery connected to the characters' childhoods.

If you want pure comedy, start with Welcome to Waikiki or The Sound of Your Heart. For romance and laughter, try Business Proposal or What's Wrong with Secretary Kim. If you prefer a story with warmth and emotional depth, Reply 1988 is a great choice.

7. Business Proposal (2022)

A blind date that turns into an office romance

Business Proposal follows Shin Ha-ri, who agrees to attend a blind date in place of her wealthy friend. Her plan is simple: act unpleasant enough to make the man lose interest.

Unfortunately, the man turns out to be Kang Tae-moo, the CEO of the company where she works. What begins as an awkward encounter becomes a series of funny misunderstandings and unexpected romantic developments.

With its exaggerated expressions, playful dialogue, office setting, and entertaining supporting characters, Business Proposal is an easy and enjoyable romantic comedy to binge-watch.

8. The Sound of Your Heart (2016)

Everyday life turned into ridiculous comedy

The Sound of Your Heart is based on the popular webtoon by Cho Seok. It follows a cartoonist and the people around him, including his family and girlfriend.

Instead of relying on dramatic plot twists, the series finds humor in ordinary situations. Family conversations, misunderstandings, strange habits, and unexpected reactions become the source of its comedy.

Its short episodes and exaggerated humor make it a good choice for viewers who want something light and easy to watch between longer dramas.

9. Fight for My Way (2017)

Friendship, big dreams, and funny everyday struggles

Fight for My Way tells the story of four young adults who are trying to build meaningful careers despite facing financial difficulties and personal setbacks.

Ko Dong-man dreams of becoming a successful fighter, while Choi Ae-ra hopes to pursue a career as a television announcer. Their friendship gradually develops into something more complicated.

The drama mixes romantic comedy with realistic themes about ambition, friendship, and the challenges of adulthood. Its funny conversations and relatable characters make it especially enjoyable.

10. Chief Kim / Good Manager (2017)

Office comedy with an unexpected hero

Chief Kim, also known as Good Manager, follows Kim Sung-ryong, an accountant who joins a major company with questionable intentions.

However, his plans change when he becomes involved in the company's internal problems and begins standing up against corruption and unfair practices.

The drama uses clever dialogue, workplace satire, funny rivalries, and unusual character interactions to create a distinctive comedy. It is a great choice for viewers who enjoy office stories with a satisfying underdog narrative.

11. Prison Playbook (2017–2018)

Unexpected friendships behind prison walls

Prison Playbook follows a famous baseball player who ends up in prison after an incident changes his life. Inside the correctional facility, he meets a variety of inmates and guards, each with their own personalities and stories.

Although the setting may sound serious, the series frequently uses humor to explore the unusual friendships and everyday situations inside the prison.

It balances comedy with emotional storytelling, showing that even in difficult circumstances, people can find friendship, hope, and moments of happiness.

12. Hospital Playlist (2020–2021)

Five doctors, lifelong friendship, and hospital life

Hospital Playlist follows five doctors who have been close friends since medical school. They work at the same hospital and share their experiences as doctors, friends, and musicians.

The humor comes from their everyday conversations, playful teasing, awkward situations, and the relationships they build with colleagues and patients.

The series is not a pure comedy because it also deals with medical challenges and emotional personal stories. However, its warm humor and strong friendships make it a comforting drama to watch.

13. The Fiery Priest (2019)

A hot-tempered priest takes on corruption

The Fiery Priest follows Kim Hae-il, a Catholic priest with a short temper and a strong sense of justice. After a suspicious death, he becomes involved in an investigation that exposes corruption and powerful people.

His unusual personality creates plenty of funny situations, especially when he works with a detective and a prosecutor who have very different approaches.

The series combines comedy, action, crime, and social satire. It is a good option for viewers who want humor alongside an exciting story.

14. Gaus Electronics (2022)

Office life has never been this chaotic

Gaus Electronics is a workplace comedy about employees working in the marketing department of a company. The characters have very different personalities, and their daily interactions often lead to ridiculous situations.

The series uses exaggerated office humor, awkward relationships, misunderstandings, and workplace frustrations to create a fast-paced comedy.

If you enjoy stories about coworkers, office drama, and funny everyday problems, Gaus Electronics is definitely worth checking out.

15. My Love from the Star (2013–2014)

An alien, a famous actress, and an unusual romance

My Love from the Star follows Do Min-joon, an alien who has lived on Earth for centuries. His quiet life changes when he becomes involved with Cheon Song-yi, a famous actress with a dramatic personality.

Their contrasting personalities create many funny moments, especially as the actress gradually becomes part of his life. The story combines romantic comedy, fantasy, mystery, and emotional drama.

Its memorable characters and unusual premise make it one of the distinctive romantic fantasy comedies in Korean television.

Which Korean Comedy Drama Should You Watch First?

The best choice depends on the kind of humor and storytelling you enjoy. Some Korean dramas focus on ridiculous situations and physical comedy, while others use romance, friendship, or workplace interactions to create laughter.

  • For non-stop laughter: Welcome to Waikiki and The Sound of Your Heart.
  • For romantic comedy: Business Proposal and What's Wrong with Secretary Kim.
  • For historical comedy: Mr. Queen.
  • For friendship and nostalgia: Reply 1988 and Hospital Playlist.
  • For school and young adult life: Weightlifting Fairy Kim Bok-joo.
  • For workplace humor: Gaus Electronics and Chief Kim.
  • For comedy with action: The Fiery Priest and Strong Woman Do Bong Soon.
  • For emotional stories with humor: Prison Playbook and Fight for My Way.

Why Korean Comedy Dramas Are So Enjoyable

One of the strengths of Korean comedy dramas is their ability to combine humor with meaningful stories. A series can make viewers laugh through silly misunderstandings while also exploring friendship, family relationships, career pressure, and personal growth.

Another reason is the variety of comedy styles. Some dramas use slapstick and exaggerated expressions, while others rely on witty dialogue, awkward romance, or relatable everyday situations.

This variety means that comedy fans do not have to watch the same type of story every time. There is always something different to discover, whether it is a chaotic guesthouse, a royal palace, a hospital, or a busy office.

Final Thoughts

Korean comedy dramas offer more than just funny scenes. They introduce memorable characters, entertaining relationships, and stories that can make viewers feel relaxed after a busy day.

If you want a series that delivers plenty of laughs, Welcome to Waikiki is a wonderful starting point. For a mix of comedy and romance, try Business Proposal or Mr. Queen. And if you want something warm and meaningful, Reply 1988 is hard to forget.

Whatever your preference, these Korean dramas are worth exploring when you need a little laughter in your day.
