15 Best Korean Romantic Dramas of All Time, with Plot Summaries and Release Dates

Published: November 5, 2026 · Updated: November 5, 2026

Korean dramas, often called K-dramas, have become a favorite form of entertainment for viewers around the world. Sweet love stories, family conflicts, personal struggles, and emotional relationships have made many Korean series unforgettable.

Some dramas tell simple love stories set in a coffee shop. Others bring together people from completely different backgrounds, while some explore romance between humans and supernatural beings. Each title has its own unique appeal.

Interestingly, many Korean dramas released years ago are still remembered and recommended by fans today. Here are 15 memorable Korean romantic dramas from different generations that deserve a place on your watchlist.

1. Winter Sonata (2002)

Original premiere date: January 14, 2002

Main cast: Bae Yong-joon and Choi Ji-woo

Genre: Romance, melodrama

Winter Sonata is one of the legendary Korean dramas that helped introduce the Korean Wave, also known as Hallyu, to audiences across Asia. The story follows Kang Joon-sang and Jung Yoo-jin, two young people who fall in love while attending school.

However, an accident causes Joon-sang to disappear, leaving Yoo-jin with painful memories. Years later, Yoo-jin meets a man who looks remarkably like her first love. Their encounter brings back an old mystery and feelings that were never truly resolved.

This drama is a great choice for viewers who enjoy first love stories, nostalgic memories, and deeply emotional romance.

2. Full House (2004)

Original premiere date: July 14, 2004

Main cast: Song Hye-kyo and Rain

Genre: Romantic comedy

Full House follows Han Ji-eun, a writer who loses her family home after being deceived by two friends. The house is eventually purchased by a famous actor named Lee Young-jae.

Hoping to get her home back, Ji-eun makes a contract marriage agreement with Young-jae. They end up living together despite having completely different personalities. Their daily arguments, jealousy, and funny situations gradually turn into genuine affection.

Full House remains a classic romantic comedy, remembered for the chemistry between its two lead actors.

3. Coffee Prince (2007)

Original premiere date: July 2, 2007

Main cast: Gong Yoo and Yoon Eun-hye

Genre: Romance, comedy

Choi Han-kyul is a wealthy young man who has little interest in managing his family's business. One day, he is asked to run a coffee shop. There, he meets Go Eun-chan, a hardworking young woman who is often mistaken for a man because of her tomboyish appearance.

Eun-chan gets a job at the coffee shop while hiding the fact that she is a woman. Han-kyul believes she is a man, but gradually becomes confused as his feelings grow stronger.

Coffee Prince is remembered for its warm and unconventional love story, as well as its themes of identity and acceptance.

4. Boys Over Flowers (2009)

Original premiere date: January 5, 2009

Main cast: Lee Min-ho and Ku Hye-sun

Genre: Romance, teen drama, comedy

Geum Jan-di is an ordinary girl who gets the opportunity to attend an elite school filled with students from wealthy families. There, she encounters F4, a group of four influential and popular male students.

One of the F4 members, Gu Jun-pyo, is known for being arrogant and stubborn. The two initially clash, but their relationship gradually develops into a complicated love story.

Differences in social status, friendship, and family interference make their journey far from easy. The drama also helped make Lee Min-ho widely recognized by international audiences.

5. Secret Garden (2010)

Original premiere date: November 13, 2010

Main cast: Hyun Bin and Ha Ji-won

Genre: Romance, fantasy, comedy

Kim Joo-won is a wealthy businessman who is arrogant and used to getting everything he wants. He meets Gil Ra-im, an ordinary woman who works as a stunt performer.

They come from completely different worlds and often argue with each other. However, a mysterious event causes their souls to switch bodies. This unusual experience forces them to understand each other's lives and feelings.

Secret Garden combines humor, fantasy, and romance in a story that keeps viewers curious about what will happen to its two main characters.

6. My Love from the Star (2013)

Original premiere date: December 18, 2013

Main cast: Kim Soo-hyun and Jun Ji-hyun

Genre: Romance, fantasy, comedy

Do Min-joon is an extraterrestrial being who has lived on Earth for approximately 400 years. He has extraordinary abilities and usually tries to keep his distance from humans.

His life changes when he meets Cheon Song-yi, a famous actress who lives nearby. Song-yi is confident, careless, and sometimes difficult to deal with, but she gradually makes Min-joon fall in love.

Their relationship faces a major obstacle because Min-joon must eventually leave Earth. This romance between a human and an alien became one of the most popular fantasy love stories in K-drama history.

7. Healer (2014)

Original premiere date: December 8, 2014

Main cast: Ji Chang-wook and Park Min-young

Genre: Romance, action, mystery

Seo Jung-hoo is a professional secret courier known by the codename Healer. He accepts dangerous assignments and does everything he can to keep his real identity hidden.

One day, a job brings him into contact with Chae Young-shin, an online reporter trying to build her career. Together, they investigate an old case that turns out to be connected to their families' past.

Amid action, secrets, and danger, their relationship develops into an engaging love story. Healer is an excellent choice for viewers who enjoy romance mixed with suspense and action.

8. Descendants of the Sun (2016)

Original premiere date: February 24, 2016

Main cast: Song Joong-ki and Song Hye-kyo

Genre: Romance, action, medical drama

Yoo Si-jin is a captain in South Korea's special forces, while Kang Mo-yeon is a dedicated doctor who works hard to save lives.

Their first meeting sparks romantic feelings, but their different professions make the relationship challenging. Si-jin faces dangerous situations as a soldier, while Mo-yeon focuses on treating and protecting her patients.

When they are assigned to the fictional country of Uruk, they meet again in situations that test their courage, loyalty, and willingness to make sacrifices.

9. Goblin (2016)

Original premiere date: December 2, 2016

Main cast: Gong Yoo and Kim Go-eun

Genre: Romance, fantasy, melodrama

Kim Shin is a goblin who has been cursed with immortality because of events in his past. To finally end his eternal life, he must find a human bride who can remove the invisible sword embedded in his body.

He eventually meets Ji Eun-tak, a young woman who can see supernatural beings and claims to be the goblin's bride. Their encounter begins a love story filled with magic, sadness, and destiny.

Alongside the main romance, the unusual friendship between the Goblin and the Grim Reaper is another memorable highlight of the series.

10. Reply 1988 (2015)

Original premiere date: November 5, 2015

Main cast: Lee Hye-ri, Park Bo-gum, Ryu Jun-yeol, Go Kyung-pyo, and Lee Dong-hwi

Genre: Romance, family, friendship

Reply 1988 takes viewers back to the Ssangmun-dong neighborhood of Seoul in 1988. The story follows five close friends growing up together in a warm and supportive community.

Alongside stories about family, friendship, and everyday life, the drama explores young love involving Sung Deok-sun and two of her male friends.

This series is about much more than romance. It also portrays relationships between parents and children, everyday struggles, and the unforgettable memories of growing up.

11. Crash Landing on You (2019)

Original premiere date: December 13, 2019

Main cast: Hyun Bin and Son Ye-jin

Genre: Romance, comedy, drama

Yoon Se-ri is a wealthy South Korean business heiress. While paragliding, she is caught in an unexpected storm and accidentally lands in North Korean territory.

There, she meets Ri Jeong-hyeok, a North Korean military officer who decides to protect her and help keep her identity hidden.

Their relationship grows despite coming from two countries with significant political and cultural differences. Crash Landing on You delivers a love story that is romantic, funny, and deeply moving.

12. Hometown Cha-Cha-Cha (2021)

Original premiere date: August 28, 2021

Main cast: Shin Min-a and Kim Seon-ho

Genre: Romance, comedy, slice of life

Yoon Hye-jin is a dentist from the big city who decides to open her own clinic in a seaside village called Gongjin.

In her new surroundings, she meets Hong Du-sik, a friendly and resourceful man who is always willing to help the local residents. Although they have very different personalities, they gradually get to know each other and fall in love.

The drama offers a warm atmosphere with beautiful coastal scenery, village life, and a romance that develops naturally.

13. Twenty-Five Twenty-One (2022)

Original premiere date: February 12, 2022

Main cast: Kim Tae-ri and Nam Joo-hyuk

Genre: Romance, coming-of-age, life drama

Set during South Korea's financial crisis in the late 1990s, this drama follows Na Hee-do, a teenager who dreams of becoming a professional fencer.

She meets Baek Yi-jin, a young man whose family is struggling financially. They support each other as they face difficulties, pursue their dreams, and experience the challenges of growing up.

Twenty-Five Twenty-One presents a sweet yet realistic story about love, friendship, ambition, and the changes that come with entering adulthood.

14. Business Proposal (2022)

Original premiere date: February 28, 2022

Main cast: Ahn Hyo-seop and Kim Se-jeong

Genre: Romantic comedy

Shin Ha-ri agrees to attend a blind date on behalf of her friend. Her plan is to make the man lose interest so that the date will end as quickly as possible.

However, things become complicated when the man turns out to be Kang Tae-moo, the CEO of the company where Ha-ri works. Even more surprisingly, Tae-moo proposes that they start a relationship and eventually get married.

Small lies, hilarious misunderstandings, and unexpected feelings make this drama a lighthearted and entertaining romantic comedy.

15. Queen of Tears (2024)

Original premiere date: March 9, 2024

Main cast: Kim Soo-hyun and Kim Ji-won

Genre: Romance, melodrama, comedy

Baek Hyun-woo comes from a modest family and works as a legal director at a major company owned by his wife's family.

He is married to Hong Hae-in, a wealthy heiress known for her strong personality and demanding nature. Unfortunately, their marriage gradually becomes filled with emotional distance and unresolved problems.

When Hae-in faces a serious health problem, the couple begins confronting feelings they have kept hidden. They try to repair their relationship and rediscover what love means within their marriage.

Queen of Tears became one of the most talked-about modern Korean dramas thanks to its mix of comedy, family conflict, and emotional romance.

♡ CewekPedia's Viewing Tips

If you are new to Korean dramas, choose a series based on your mood. Want something funny? Try Full House or Business Proposal. In the mood for fantasy romance? Give Goblin or My Love from the Star a try. If you prefer emotional love stories, Winter Sonata, Crash Landing on You, and Queen of Tears are worth adding to your watchlist.

Which Korean Romantic Dramas Are Worth Rewatching?

Every drama on this list has something special to offer. Classic series bring nostalgia and simple love stories, while newer productions explore a wider range of relationships and modern life experiences.

If you want to choose based on the type of story, here are a few recommendations:

  • Classic romance: Winter Sonata and Full House.
  • Romantic comedy: Coffee Prince and Business Proposal.
  • Fantasy romance: Goblin and My Love from the Star.
  • Love against the odds: Descendants of the Sun and Crash Landing on You.
  • Emotional love stories: Twenty-Five Twenty-One and Queen of Tears.
  • Family and friendship: Reply 1988.

Final Thoughts

Korean romantic dramas are about more than two people falling in love. Many of them also explore personal struggles, sacrifice, friendship, family, and the journey of discovering who we really are.

These 15 titles represent different generations of Korean television and have each earned a special place in the hearts of K-drama fans. Even years after their original release, many of their stories remain enjoyable to watch again.

So, which one is your favorite? Are you on Team Goblin, Crash Landing on You, or Queen of Tears? Or do you still have a soft spot for the classic Winter Sonata?
