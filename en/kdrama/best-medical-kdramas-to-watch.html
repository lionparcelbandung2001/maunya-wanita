Top 7 Best Medical K-Dramas to Watch: Heartwarming & Thrilling!

Published: June 1, 2026 · Updated: June 1, 2026

For K-drama fans, medical series always hold a special place in the heart. Beyond the intense operating room scenes, these shows often weave beautiful stories of deep friendship, romance, and the emotional struggles of healthcare professionals saving lives.

Here is a curated list of top-rated medical K-dramas complete with synopses, cast details, and release dates that deserve a spot on your watch list!

1. Hospital Playlist (2020 - 2021)

Directed by Shin Won-ho, this phenomenal series follows five close doctor friends who entered medical school together in 1999. Working at Yulje Medical Center, they share a special bond and form a band together in their spare time.

  • Release Date: March 12, 2020 (Season 1) & June 17, 2021 (Season 2)
  • Main Cast: Jo Jung-suk, Yoo Yeon-seok, Jung Kyung-ho, Kim Dae-myung, Jeon Mi-do

Why Watch Hospital Playlist

Rather than dark hospital politics, this drama focuses on heartwarming human connections, patient stories, and the inspiring dedication of compassionate doctors.

♡ CewekPedia Tips

Grab some tissues and your favorite snacks before watching Hospital Playlist—it will make you shed tears of emotion while smiling warmly!

2. Dr. Romantic / Romantic Doctor, Teacher Kim (2016 - 2023)

This gripping series tells the story of Boo Yong-joo, a genius surgeon known as "Teacher Kim" who isolates himself and works at a rundown countryside hospital called Doldam. There, he mentors ambitious young doctors to become medical professionals with true integrity.

  • Release Date: November 7, 2016 (Season 1), January 6, 2020 (Season 2), & April 28, 2023 (Season 3)
  • Main Cast: Han Suk-kyu, Yoo Yeon-seok, Seo Hyun-jin, Ahn Hyo-seop, Lee Sung-kyung

3. Doctor Slump (2024)

A medical romantic comedy following two former high school academic rivals who reunite as successful plastic surgeons. When both hit major slumps (facing burnout and professional crises) in their lives, they accidentally cross paths again and help each other heal.

  • Release Date: January 27, 2024 – March 17, 2024
  • Main Cast: Park Hyung-sik, Park Shin-hye, Yoon Park, Kong Seong-ha

Conclusion

These phenomenal medical K-dramas offer both thrilling entertainment and deep human values. Which one is your absolute favorite or going straight to your weekend watch list?
