15 Best Korean Time-Travel Dramas of All Time: Must-Watch K-Dramas

Published: November 13, 2026 · Updated: November 13, 2026

What if you could return to the past, meet someone before your lives changed, or get another chance to make a different decision? This fascinating idea is one reason time-travel stories have become such an entertaining part of Korean drama storytelling.

Korean time-travel dramas come in many forms. Some combine romance with historical settings, while others focus on crime investigations, family relationships, fantasy, or changing the future. Here are 15 memorable K-dramas to explore if you enjoy stories that connect different periods of time.

15 Best Korean Time-Travel Dramas to Watch

1. Signal (2016)

Signal is a gripping crime drama about detectives living in different time periods who communicate through a mysterious walkie-talkie. Their conversations help them investigate cold cases and attempt to prevent tragedies that have not yet happened. With its emotional storytelling and carefully connected mysteries, Signal is an excellent choice for viewers who enjoy intelligent time-related crime stories.

2. Moon Lovers: Scarlet Heart Ryeo (2016)

After a solar eclipse, a modern woman finds herself transported to the Goryeo Dynasty, where she wakes up in the body of a young woman named Hae Soo. She becomes involved in the lives and political struggles of several royal princes. Moon Lovers combines historical intrigue, romance, friendship, and heartbreak in a story where the future is far from certain.

3. Mr. Queen (2020–2021)

A modern-day chef unexpectedly finds his consciousness inside the body of Queen Cheorin during Korea's Joseon era. As he tries to understand palace life and survive royal politics, his modern personality creates plenty of unexpected situations. Mr. Queen is a fun combination of historical comedy, romance, and fantasy with a distinctive body-swap premise.

4. Lovely Runner (2024)

Lovely Runner follows Im Sol, a devoted fan who gets an extraordinary opportunity to travel back to the past after the tragic fate of her favorite celebrity. She returns to an earlier period in an attempt to protect him and change the future. The drama blends romance, youthful memories, suspense, and emotional twists into a heartfelt time-travel story.

5. Twinkling Watermelon (2023)

A talented teenager who grows up in a family with hearing difficulties suddenly travels back to 1995. There, he meets his father as a teenager and becomes involved in events that could change his family's future. Twinkling Watermelon combines music, friendship, family relationships, and a touching coming-of-age story.

♡ CewekPedia Tips

If you are new to time-travel K-dramas, start with Lovely Runner for romance, Mr. Queen for comedy, or Signal for mystery. Each one offers a different way to explore the idea of changing the past.

6. Go Back Couple (2017)

An unhappy married couple suddenly finds themselves back in their university days, when they were still young and had not yet built their life together. Given another chance to experience the past, both begin reconsidering their choices and relationships. Go Back Couple is an emotional story about marriage, regret, family, and second chances.

7. Tunnel (2017)

While chasing a serial killer in 1986, a detective runs through a tunnel and mysteriously arrives 30 years in the future. The crimes he was investigating are still connected to the present, forcing him to adapt to a completely different world. Tunnel is a strong choice for viewers who enjoy crime investigations mixed with time travel.

8. Rooftop Prince (2012)

A Joseon-era crown prince and his companions suddenly find themselves transported to modern-day Seoul. Confused by the unfamiliar world, they encounter a woman whose life becomes connected to the prince's mysterious past. Rooftop Prince mixes romantic comedy, historical mystery, and the contrast between royal life and modern society.

9. Tomorrow, with You (2017)

A successful businessman discovers that he can travel through time using the subway. After seeing glimpses of an uncertain future, he makes decisions that affect his relationship and his own fate. Tomorrow, with You offers a more mature romantic story, exploring love, destiny, and the difficult consequences of knowing what may happen.

10. Splash Splash Love (2015)

A high school student who feels overwhelmed by her studies unexpectedly travels back to the Joseon Dynasty. There, she meets a young king and becomes involved in royal life using the knowledge she has gained from the modern world. This short drama is a charming mix of fantasy, comedy, and romance.

11. My Perfect Stranger (2023)

A journalist and a publishing employee travel back to 1987, where their separate goals become connected. One wants to investigate a series of murders, while the other hopes to understand her family's past. My Perfect Stranger combines a time-travel mystery with crime investigation and emotional family storytelling.

12. A Time Called You (2023)

After losing someone she deeply loves, a woman mysteriously finds herself in 1998, living as a high school student who looks different but has a familiar connection to her past. As she meets people who resemble those she remembers, the mystery of identity, memory, and time begins to unfold. This series is a romantic mystery with a complex timeline.

13. Reborn Rich (2022)

After being betrayed and killed, a loyal employee of a powerful business family wakes up in the body of the family's youngest grandson in an earlier period. Armed with memories of the future, he tries to change his destiny and gain control of the family empire. Reborn Rich combines corporate rivalry, revenge, and a second-chance timeline.

14. Marry My Husband (2024)

After experiencing betrayal and a tragic ending, Kang Ji-won wakes up ten years in the past with memories of her previous life. She decides to change the course of her future and take control of the relationships that once caused her suffering. Marry My Husband uses time regression as the foundation for a satisfying revenge and second-chance story.

15. Bon Appétit, Your Majesty (2025)

A talented modern chef finds herself transported to the Joseon royal court, where her cooking skills become essential to surviving palace life. Her modern approach to food creates unexpected encounters with a demanding king and the people around him. This fantasy historical drama offers a lighter time-travel adventure with food, palace politics, and romance.

Which Time-Travel K-Drama Should You Watch First?

The best choice depends on the type of story you enjoy. Some dramas focus on romance and emotional second chances, while others use time travel to create mysteries, historical adventures, or revenge stories.

  • For romance: Lovely Runner, Moon Lovers: Scarlet Heart Ryeo, and Tomorrow, with You.
  • For comedy and fantasy: Mr. Queen, Rooftop Prince, and Splash Splash Love.
  • For crime and mystery: Signal, Tunnel, and My Perfect Stranger.
  • For family and emotional stories: Twinkling Watermelon and Go Back Couple.
  • For revenge and second chances: Reborn Rich and Marry My Husband.
  • For historical fantasy: Moon Lovers and Bon Appétit, Your Majesty.

Why Are Time-Travel Stories So Popular in Korean Dramas?

Time travel gives writers an interesting way to explore regret, destiny, love, and personal growth. A character who returns to the past may discover that changing one event can affect many other lives. This creates emotional choices that are just as important as the fantasy concept itself.

Korean dramas also use time travel in many different ways. Some feature characters physically moving between historical periods, while others explore repeated lives, altered timelines, or communication between people living in different years. This variety allows the genre to combine romance, comedy, mystery, and science fiction.

It is worth noting that not every title on this list uses traditional time travel. Some use time regression, time slips, or related fantasy devices. They are included because they share the central appeal of revisiting the past and changing what happens next.

Final Thoughts

From the emotional romance of Lovely Runner to the mystery of Signal and the historical comedy of Mr. Queen, Korean time-travel dramas offer something for almost every type of viewer.

Whether you want a story about changing destiny, meeting someone in another era, or solving a mystery across decades, these 15 dramas are a great starting point. Pick the theme that interests you most and get ready for a journey through time.

This list is a curated selection based on storytelling, genre variety, and popularity among K-drama viewers. It is not an official ranking, and individual preferences may differ.
