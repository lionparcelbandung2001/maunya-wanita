6 Best Korean Historical War Dramas: Storylines, Cast & Awards

Published: 2026-11-08 · Updated: 2026-11-08

Korean historical war dramas bring together battlefield strategy, political rivalries, royal ambition and stories of national survival. Some are inspired by major conflicts such as the Goryeo–Khitan wars and the Imjin War, while others focus on the military and political struggles surrounding the founding of Joseon.

This guide features six Korean period series, with premiere dates, episode counts, principal cast members and notable verified awards. The list includes large-scale war sagas as well as historical action dramas, so each title is described according to the kind of conflict it actually portrays.

The Korean Historical War Drama List

1. Korea-Khitan War (2023–2024)

Original premiere: 11 November 2023  |  Episodes: 32 episode

Synopsis

Set during the Goryeo dynasty, this series follows King Hyeonjong and his commanders as they try to unite the kingdom against Khitan invasions. Military strategy, diplomacy, sacrifice and major battles shape the story.

Main cast

Choi Soo-jong as Gang Gam-chan; Kim Dong-jun as King Hyeonjong; Ji Seung-hyun as Yang Gyu.

Notable awards

At the 2023 KBS Drama Awards, Choi Soo-jong won the Grand Prize (Daesang), Kim Dong-jun received a Top Excellence Award, and Ji Seung-hyun won an Excellence Award for a long-form drama. The production also received several other awards that evening.

Why watch it?

A strong choice for viewers who want large land battles, military strategy and a story about defending a kingdom.

2. Immortal Admiral Yi Sun-sin (2004–2005)

Original premiere: 4 September 2004  |  Episodes: 104 episode

Synopsis

This historical epic follows Yi Sun-sin, the Joseon naval commander who led resistance against Japanese forces during the late sixteenth century. The story traces his development as a leader and the naval campaigns associated with the Imjin War, including the famous turtle ships.

Main cast

Kim Myung-min as Admiral Yi Sun-sin; Choi Jae-sung as Won Gyun; Lee Jae-ryong as Ryu Seong-ryong; Choi Cheol-ho as King Seonjo.

Notable awards

At the 2005 KBS Drama Awards, Kim Myung-min won the Grand Prize (Daesang) for his portrayal of Yi Sun-sin, and Park Chul-min received a supporting actor award. Kim Myung-min also won Best Actor at the 2005 Grimae Awards.

Why watch it?

Best for viewers interested in naval warfare, admirals, military tactics and a real historical figure.

3. The Jingbirok: A Memoir of Imjin War (2015)

Original premiere: 14 Februari 2015  |  Episodes: 50 episode

Synopsis

Inspired by the memoir of scholar-official Ryu Seong-ryong, the series depicts Joseon court politics before and during the Japanese invasions of 1592–1598. It follows the government’s response to the crisis through the final Battle of Noryang.

Main cast

Kim Sang-joong as Ryu Seong-ryong; Kim Tae-woo as King Seonjo; Lim Dong-jin and Sun Dong-hyuk in important supporting roles.

Notable awards

At the 2015 KBS Drama Awards, Kim Tae-woo won an Excellence Award for a serial drama and Kim Kyu-cheol received a Supporting Actor Award. Kim Sang-joong was nominated in an acting category; that nomination should not be mistaken for a win.

Why watch it?

A good choice for viewers who want the political and administrative side of the Imjin War, not only battlefield scenes.

4. Empress Ki (2013–2014)

Original premiere: 28 Oktober 2013  |  Episodes: 51 episode

Synopsis

Inspired by the life of a Goryeo-born woman who rose to become an empress of the Yuan dynasty, the series combines palace intrigue, political maneuvering, military conflict and a struggle for influence. Its story is dramatized rather than a documentary account.

Main cast

Ha Ji-won as Ki Seung-nyang; Ji Chang-wook as Toghon Temür; Joo Jin-mo as Wang Yoo; Baek Jin-hee as Tanashiri.

Notable awards

At the 2013 MBC Drama Awards, Ha Ji-won won the Grand Prize (Daesang), while Joo Jin-mo and Ji Chang-wook received acting awards and Baek Jin-hee won a newcomer award. Empress Ki also won the Golden Bird Prize for Best Series Drama at the 2014 Seoul International Drama Awards.

Why watch it?

Best suited to viewers who enjoy palace intrigue, imperial politics, a central female character and conflicts over power.

5. Six Flying Dragons (2015–2016)

Original premiere: 5 Oktober 2015  |  Episodes: 50 episode

Synopsis

Set during the final years of Goryeo, the drama follows six figures whose ambitions and conflicts shape the founding of Joseon. Sword fights, political strategy and factional rivalries drive the story of a new dynasty taking form.

Main cast

Yoo Ah-in as Lee Bang-won; Kim Myung-min as Jeong Do-jeon; Shin Se-kyung as Boon-yi; Byun Yo-han as Lee Bang-ji; Yoon Kyun-sang as Moo-hyul; Chun Ho-jin as Lee Seong-gye.

Notable awards

Among its notable honours, Yoo Ah-in won Best Actor in the television category at the 2016 Baeksang Arts Awards, and the series won Best Series Drama at the 2016 Seoul International Drama Awards.

Why watch it?

A strong pick for sword action, political strategy and the turbulent transition between dynasties.

6. Warrior Baek Dong-soo (2011)

Original premiere: 4 Juli 2011  |  Episodes: 29 episode

Synopsis

Set in the Joseon era, this action period drama follows Baek Dong-soo as he becomes a renowned swordsman. Friendship, loyalty, rival fighters, an assassin group and political intrigue shape the plot. It features more martial-arts action than large-scale warfare.

Main cast

Ji Chang-wook as Baek Dong-soo; Yoo Seung-ho as Yeo Woon; Yoon So-yi as Hwang Jin-joo; Shin Hyun-bin as Yoo Ji-seon; Choi Min-soo as Cheon.

Notable awards

Choi Min-soo received a nomination at the 2011 SBS Drama Awards. The sources checked for this article did not establish a major award win for the series, so the nomination is not presented as a win.

Why watch it?

Ideal for viewers who prefer sword duels and martial-arts action over large-scale battles between kingdoms.

How to Choose the Right Series

For large-scale military campaigns, start with Korea-Khitan War or Immortal Admiral Yi Sun-sin. The Jingbirok offers a closer look at the political decisions surrounding the Imjin War. Empress Ki and Six Flying Dragons focus more on power struggles, while Warrior Baek Dong-soo is best suited to viewers who enjoy sword-fighting action.

♡ Tips CewekPedia

Check the episode count and storytelling style before choosing a series. Historical Korean dramas often combine documented figures and events with fictionalized scenes. Treat them as entertainment inspired by history rather than as documentary accounts.

A Note on Historical Accuracy and Awards

Note: historical dramas are works of dramatization. Real people and events may be adapted or simplified for storytelling. The awards below list verifiable wins; nominations are identified separately.
