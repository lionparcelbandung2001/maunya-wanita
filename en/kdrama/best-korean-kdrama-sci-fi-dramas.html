15 Best Korean kdrama & Sci-Fi Dramas of All Time

Published: November 13, 2026 · Updated: November 13, 2026

Korean dramas are famous for their romance, emotional storytelling, and unforgettable characters. However, the Korean entertainment industry has also created some extraordinary kdrama and science fiction series that transport viewers into completely different worlds.

From immortal beings and powerful sorcerers to time-traveling detectives, parallel universes, mysterious aliens, and supernatural creatures, these K-dramas offer much more than ordinary television stories. Some combine kdrama with romance, while others explore dark mysteries, action, horror, and futuristic technology.

If you are looking for something imaginative and exciting to watch, here are 15 Korean kdrama and sci-fi dramas that deserve a place on your watchlist.

1. Guardian: The Lonely and Great God (Goblin) (2016)

Often simply called Goblin, this legendary kdrama romance follows Kim Shin, an immortal warrior cursed to live for centuries as a goblin. His extraordinary existence changes when he meets a young woman who may hold the key to ending his eternal life.

The series combines Korean mythology, supernatural beings, romance, comedy, and emotional storytelling. The relationship between the goblin and the grim reaper is also one of its most memorable elements.

With beautiful cinematography, a remarkable soundtrack, and an unforgettable cast, Goblin remains one of the most recognizable Korean kdrama dramas.

Genre: kdrama, Romance, Supernatural, Comedy

Episodes: 16

2. Alchemy of Souls (2022–2023)

Set in a fictional kingdom where powerful sorcerers practice magical arts, Alchemy of Souls introduces a world filled with ancient spells, political conflicts, powerful families, and extraordinary abilities.

The story follows Jang Uk, a young nobleman whose destiny becomes connected to a powerful assassin named Naksu. Through a mysterious soul-shifting spell, Naksu finds herself trapped inside another woman's body.

As their relationship develops, they become involved in dangerous magical conflicts that threaten the entire kingdom.

The series stands out for its imaginative world-building, magical battles, character development, and impressive production design.

Genre: kdrama, Action, Romance, Historical

Episodes: 30 across two parts

3. Hotel Del Luna (2019)

What if there were a hotel that ordinary living people could never see? Hotel Del Luna explores exactly that idea.

The hotel is a mysterious establishment that serves the souls of deceased people before they move on to the afterlife. It is managed by Jang Man-wol, a beautiful but mysterious woman who has been bound to the hotel for centuries.

Everything changes when a human hotel manager becomes involved in its supernatural operations.

The drama offers beautiful visual effects, mysterious spirits, emotional stories, and a fascinating world between life and death.

Genre: kdrama, Supernatural, Romance, Drama

Episodes: 16

4. My Love from the Star (2013–2014)

This popular Korean science fiction romance follows Do Min-joon, an extraterrestrial visitor who has lived on Earth for approximately 400 years.

Although he possesses extraordinary abilities, including enhanced senses and unusual powers, he tries to live quietly among humans while waiting for the opportunity to return home.

His peaceful life becomes complicated when he meets Cheon Song-yi, a famous actress whose personality is completely different from his own.

The combination of alien science fiction, comedy, romance, and emotional moments makes this series a memorable classic.

Genre: Sci-Fi, kdrama, Romance, Comedy

Episodes: 21

♡ CewekPedia Tips

If you enjoy kdrama stories with emotional romance, start with Goblin, Hotel Del Luna, or My Love from the Star. If you prefer magical battles and larger fictional worlds, Alchemy of Souls is an excellent choice.

5. Signal (2016)

Signal is one of the most fascinating Korean dramas combining science fiction and crime investigation.

The story revolves around a mysterious walkie-talkie that allows a modern-day criminal profiler to communicate with a detective from the past.

By exchanging information across different timelines, they attempt to solve cold cases and prevent tragic events. However, changing the past creates unexpected consequences in the present.

Its gripping mysteries, intelligent storytelling, and emotional tension make Signal a must-watch for fans of time-related science fiction.

Genre: Sci-Fi, Crime, Mystery, Thriller

Episodes: 16

6. W: Two Worlds (2016)

Imagine falling in love with someone who exists inside a comic book. W: Two Worlds turns this unusual idea into an exciting kdrama science fiction story.

Oh Yeon-joo, a surgeon, unexpectedly enters the fictional world created by her father. There, she meets Kang Chul, the main character of a popular webtoon.

As the boundaries between reality and fiction begin to disappear, both worlds become increasingly dangerous.

The series offers an unusual premise, unexpected twists, romance, and suspenseful storytelling.

Genre: kdrama, Sci-Fi, Romance, Thriller

Episodes: 16

7. Moving (2023)

Moving tells the story of teenagers who inherit extraordinary superhuman abilities from their parents.

Some can fly, others possess incredible strength, and some have unusual healing abilities. However, their powers also attract dangerous attention from people who want to use them for their own purposes.

The story gradually reveals the complicated pasts of their parents and the sacrifices they made to protect their children.

Moving successfully combines superhero action, family relationships, mystery, and emotional storytelling. It is one of the standout Korean series for viewers who enjoy superhuman science fiction.

Genre: Sci-Fi, Superhero, Action, Mystery

Episodes: 20

8. Death's Game (2023–2024)

Death's Game explores a dark kdrama concept involving life, death, and the consequences of human decisions.

After reaching a desperate point in his life, Choi Yi-jae encounters Death, who gives him a terrifying challenge: experience death repeatedly by living through the final moments of different people.

Each new life introduces a different identity, situation, and challenge. However, the experience becomes much more complicated than he initially expects.

With an unusual premise and several interconnected stories, this series is an intense exploration of mortality and the value of life.

Genre: kdrama, Thriller, Supernatural, Drama

Episodes: 8

9. Kingdom (2019–2020)

Kingdom takes viewers to Korea's Joseon era, where a mysterious disease transforms people into terrifying creatures.

While investigating the strange outbreak, Crown Prince Lee Chang discovers a conspiracy that threatens the kingdom.

The drama combines historical political intrigue, survival horror, supernatural mystery, and intense action sequences.

Its unique historical setting and impressive production make Kingdom an outstanding choice for viewers who enjoy dark kdrama and zombie stories.

Genre: Historical kdrama, Horror, Thriller, Action

Episodes: 12 across two seasons, plus a special episode

10. Sweet Home (2020–2024)

Sweet Home presents a terrifying world in which humans begin transforming into monsters based on their deepest desires.

The story follows Cha Hyun-su, a troubled young man who finds himself trapped inside an apartment building as the world outside descends into chaos.

Alongside other survivors, he must confront terrifying creatures while struggling with the mysterious changes happening inside himself.

The series combines horror, kdrama, action, psychological drama, and disturbing monster designs.

Genre: Horror, kdrama, Sci-Fi, Action

Episodes: 26 across three seasons

11. The Uncanny Counter (2020–2023)

The Uncanny Counter follows a group of ordinary people who secretly work as demon hunters.

Disguised as employees of a noodle restaurant, the group uses extraordinary abilities to track down evil spirits that threaten human beings.

Each member has unique strengths, and their teamwork becomes essential as they face increasingly dangerous supernatural enemies.

The drama is an entertaining mixture of action, comedy, friendship, emotional moments, and supernatural battles.

Genre: kdrama, Action, Supernatural, Mystery

Episodes: 28 across two seasons

12. Tale of the Nine Tailed (2020)

Inspired by Korean folklore, Tale of the Nine Tailed introduces a mythical nine-tailed fox known as a gumiho.

Lee Yeon lives among humans while dealing with supernatural creatures and unresolved events from his past.

His life becomes complicated when a television producer begins investigating mysterious supernatural incidents connected to him.

The series blends Korean mythology, romance, kdrama action, and mystery into an entertaining supernatural adventure.

Genre: kdrama, Romance, Supernatural, Action

Episodes: 16 in the first season

13. The King: Eternal Monarch (2020)

What if two parallel worlds existed side by side, separated by a mysterious gateway?

The King: Eternal Monarch explores this concept through the story of a Korean emperor who discovers a passage connecting two different realities.

As he crosses between worlds, he encounters a detective from another version of Korea. Together, they become involved in a dangerous mystery that threatens both universes.

The drama combines parallel-world science fiction, kdrama, romance, and political intrigue.

Genre: kdrama, Sci-Fi, Romance, Mystery

Episodes: 16

14. Twinkling Watermelon (2023)

Twinkling Watermelon offers a refreshing take on time travel, music, and family relationships.

The story follows Ha Eun-gyeol, a talented young musician who unexpectedly travels back to 1995.

There, he meets a younger version of his father and becomes involved in events that could change the future of his family.

Rather than focusing on complicated scientific explanations, the drama uses its time-travel premise to explore communication, friendship, dreams, and the emotional connections between parents and children.

Genre: kdrama, Time Travel, Music, Romance

Episodes: 16

15. Nine: Nine Times Time Travel (2013)

For fans of classic Korean science fiction, Nine: Nine Times Time Travel deserves special attention.

The series follows Park Sun-woo, a news anchor who discovers nine mysterious incense sticks that allow him to travel twenty years into the past.

At first, he believes he can use them to correct painful events and improve the lives of people around him. However, every change to the past creates unexpected consequences.

Its clever time-travel concept, suspenseful plot, and exploration of cause and effect make it a particularly memorable science fiction drama.

Genre: Sci-Fi, kdrama, Mystery, Thriller

Episodes: 20

How to Choose the Right Korean kdrama or Sci-Fi Drama

Not every kdrama or science fiction drama offers the same experience. Some focus on magical worlds, while others explore complicated timelines, supernatural mysteries, or futuristic concepts.

Here are some recommendations based on your preferred storytelling style:

  • For magical worlds: Alchemy of Souls and Goblin.
  • For supernatural mysteries: Hotel Del Luna and Tale of the Nine Tailed.
  • For time travel: Signal, Twinkling Watermelon, and Nine.
  • For parallel universes: W: Two Worlds and The King: Eternal Monarch.
  • For superheroes and special abilities: Moving and The Uncanny Counter.
  • For dark kdrama and horror: Kingdom and Sweet Home.
  • For emotional science fiction: My Love from the Star and Death's Game.

Final Thoughts

Korean kdrama and science fiction dramas have proven that great storytelling does not have to be limited by reality.

Whether it is an immortal goblin searching for freedom, a detective communicating across time, a teenager discovering extraordinary powers, or a mysterious gateway connecting parallel worlds, these stories offer something special for different kinds of viewers.

The 15 dramas on this list represent a variety of imaginative concepts and storytelling styles. Some are romantic and emotional, others are dark and mysterious, while several deliver spectacular action and supernatural adventures.

If you have never explored Korean kdrama or sci-fi dramas before, Goblin, Alchemy of Souls, Moving, and Signal are excellent places to begin.
