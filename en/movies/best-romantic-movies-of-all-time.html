12 Best Romantic Movies of All Time That Will Make You Fall in Love, with Summaries and Release Dates

Published: November 5, 2026 · Updated: November 5, 2026

Romantic movies have a special place in the hearts of film lovers. Some stories make us smile, others make us laugh, and some bring tears to our eyes as we follow two people navigating the ups and downs of love.

Hollywood and other Western film industries have produced countless love stories that continue to be remembered across generations. Some of these movies are still watched again and again, even decades after their original release.

If you are looking for a romantic movie to enjoy during your free time, here are 12 of the best romantic movies of all time. This list includes timeless classics, emotional love stories, romantic comedies and modern favourites.

1. Casablanca (1942)

Original premiere: November 26, 1942, in New York; general US release on January 23, 1943.

Cast: Humphrey Bogart, Ingrid Bergman, Paul Henreid.

Genre: Drama, romance, war.

Casablanca is one of the most famous romantic classics in cinema history. The story follows Rick Blaine, the owner of a nightclub in Morocco during World War II.

Rick's life takes an unexpected turn when Ilsa Lund, the woman he once loved, arrives with her husband, Victor Laszlo. Their reunion brings back old memories and forces Rick to face a difficult choice between personal happiness and sacrifice.

Casablanca is more than a story about two people in love. It also explores the idea that sometimes love means letting someone go for a greater cause.

2. Roman Holiday (1953)

Release date: September 2, 1953.

Cast: Audrey Hepburn, Gregory Peck, Eddie Albert.

Genre: Romantic comedy.

Roman Holiday tells the story of Princess Ann, a royal who feels exhausted by official duties and palace rules. During a visit to Rome, she secretly escapes to experience life as an ordinary young woman.

In the city, she meets Joe Bradley, an American journalist who initially sees their encounter as an opportunity to get an exclusive story. However, as they explore Rome together, the two begin to grow closer.

This film offers a sweet romantic atmosphere, beautiful views of Rome and a simple love story that stays with the audience.

3. When Harry Met Sally... (1989)

Release date: July 21, 1989, in the United States.

Cast: Billy Crystal, Meg Ryan, Carrie Fisher.

Genre: Romantic comedy.

Can men and women remain close friends without eventually falling in love? That question lies at the heart of When Harry Met Sally.

Harry and Sally first meet during a road trip to New York. They have different opinions about relationships, love and friendship. After going their separate ways, they meet again several times and gradually build a close friendship.

As time passes, their growing connection makes them question whether their feelings have become something more. This movie is a great choice for anyone who enjoys witty dialogue and light-hearted romance.

4. Pretty Woman (1990)

Release date: March 23, 1990.

Cast: Julia Roberts, Richard Gere, Ralph Bellamy.

Genre: Romantic comedy.

Pretty Woman follows Edward Lewis, a wealthy businessman who meets Vivian Ward in Los Angeles. What begins as a business arrangement gradually develops into a much closer relationship.

Edward invites Vivian to accompany him to several social events. As they spend more time together, they discover each other's personal sides and realise that their connection is no longer just an agreement.

The film is widely remembered for its modern Cinderella-style story and Julia Roberts' iconic performance.

5. Titanic (1997)

Release date: December 19, 1997.

Cast: Leonardo DiCaprio, Kate Winslet, Billy Zane.

Genre: Drama, romance, disaster.

Titanic is one of the most popular romantic films in the world. The story follows Rose DeWitt Bukater, a young woman from a wealthy family travelling aboard the RMS Titanic.

During the voyage, Rose meets Jack Dawson, a young man from a modest background who wins his ticket unexpectedly. Despite their different social positions, the two develop strong feelings for each other.

Their romance takes a tragic turn when the Titanic strikes an iceberg. The story of Jack and Rose has become a memorable symbol of love, courage and unforgettable memories.

6. Notting Hill (1999)

Release dates: May 21, 1999, in the United Kingdom; May 28, 1999, in the United States.

Cast: Julia Roberts, Hugh Grant, Rhys Ifans.

Genre: Romantic comedy.

William Thacker owns a small bookshop in the London neighbourhood of Notting Hill. His ordinary life changes when Anna Scott, a famous Hollywood actress, walks into his shop.

Their simple encounter develops into a romantic relationship. However, their very different lifestyles make things complicated. William must deal with public attention, Anna's busy career and several misunderstandings.

Notting Hill is a warm love story about two people from completely different worlds trying to find a place in each other's lives.

7. Love Actually (2003)

Original release date: November 7, 2003, in the United Kingdom.

Cast: Hugh Grant, Emma Thompson, Colin Firth, Keira Knightley, Liam Neeson.

Genre: Romantic comedy, drama.

Unlike romantic movies that focus on just one couple, Love Actually presents several interconnected love stories taking place in London in the weeks before Christmas.

The stories include a prime minister falling for a member of his staff, a writer discovering love in an unexpected place and a married couple facing challenges in their relationship.

Each storyline shows that love can take many forms. It can be new romance, family affection, friendship or feelings that remain unspoken.

8. The Notebook (2004)

Release date: June 25, 2004.

Cast: Ryan Gosling, Rachel McAdams, James Garner, Gena Rowlands.

Genre: Romantic drama.

The Notebook tells the story of Noah Calhoun and Allie Hamilton, two young people who fall in love despite coming from very different social backgrounds.

Their relationship does not receive approval from Allie's family. Circumstances eventually separate them, but Noah never truly forgets his feelings for her.

Years later, fate brings them together again. The story is told through memories read from a notebook, creating a moving portrait of loyalty, love and the passage of time.

9. Pride & Prejudice (2005)

Release dates: September 16, 2005, in the United Kingdom; November 23, 2005, in the United States.

Cast: Keira Knightley, Matthew Macfadyen, Rosamund Pike.

Genre: Romantic drama, historical.

Based on the famous novel by Jane Austen, Pride & Prejudice follows Elizabeth Bennet and her family. Elizabeth is an intelligent young woman with strong opinions about love and marriage.

She meets Mr. Darcy, a wealthy man who initially appears proud and difficult to approach. Their unpleasant first impression leads them to misunderstand each other repeatedly.

Over time, however, they begin to see each other's personalities more clearly. This film is an excellent choice for viewers who enjoy classic romance set in historical England.

10. About Time (2013)

Release date: August 8, 2013, in the United Kingdom.

Cast: Domhnall Gleeson, Rachel McAdams, Bill Nighy.

Genre: Romantic comedy, fantasy, drama.

Tim Lake discovers that the men in his family have the ability to travel back in time. He begins using this extraordinary gift to change certain moments in his life.

When Tim meets Mary, he tries to build a happy relationship with her. However, he gradually learns that changing the past does not always make life perfect.

About Time is not only a film about romantic love. It is also a story about family, time and the importance of appreciating ordinary moments.

11. La La Land (2016)

Limited release: December 9, 2016, in the United States; wide release on December 25, 2016.

Cast: Emma Stone, Ryan Gosling, John Legend.

Genre: Musical, romantic drama.

La La Land follows Mia, an aspiring actress, and Sebastian, a jazz musician who dreams of opening his own music club.

They meet in Los Angeles and begin a relationship. They support each other's ambitions, but the journey towards success brings unexpected challenges to their romance.

With beautiful music, colourful visuals and an emotional storyline, La La Land has become one of the most talked-about modern romantic films.

12. Dirty Dancing (1987)

Release date: August 21, 1987.

Cast: Jennifer Grey, Patrick Swayze, Jerry Orbach.

Genre: Romantic drama, musical.

Dirty Dancing tells the story of Frances "Baby" Houseman, a young woman spending her summer holiday with her family at a resort.

There, she meets Johnny Castle, a dance instructor. Baby becomes interested in learning to dance and gradually grows closer to Johnny.

Their relationship faces challenges involving social class, other people's opinions and personal problems. The film is famous for combining romance, music and energetic dance performances.

♡ CewekPedia Movie Tips

If you want a light and enjoyable romantic movie, start with Pretty Woman, Notting Hill or When Harry Met Sally. For more emotional stories, try The Notebook, Titanic or La La Land. Meanwhile, Pride & Prejudice and Roman Holiday are wonderful choices if you enjoy classic romance.

Which Romantic Movie Should You Watch First?

Every film has its own charm and atmosphere. Not every love story needs a happy ending to leave a lasting impression. Some movies are entertaining, others explore sacrifice, and some remind us to appreciate the people closest to us.

  • For classic love stories: Casablanca and Roman Holiday.
  • For romantic comedies: Pretty Woman, Notting Hill and When Harry Met Sally.
  • For emotional romances: Titanic and The Notebook.
  • For romance and music: La La Land and Dirty Dancing.
  • For historical romance: Pride & Prejudice.
  • For love and family themes: About Time.

Final Thoughts

The best romantic movies of all time are about more than two people falling in love. They also explore differences, sacrifice, friendship, family, dreams and second chances.

From Casablanca to La La Land, each film offers a different perspective on love. It is no surprise that many of these movies continue to be remembered and enjoyed by audiences across generations.
