Hollywood Movies Coming in November and December 2026: Release Dates & Guide

Published: 6 November 2026 · Updated: 6 November 2026

Movie fans have plenty to look forward to as 2026 comes to a close. November and December bring a varied lineup of Hollywood releases, including animated adventures, family comedies, fantasy stories, action films and major science-fiction blockbusters.

This guide highlights selected upcoming movies, their announced U.S. release dates, genres, casts and available story details. Release dates may vary across the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia and other international markets.

Upcoming Hollywood movies in November 2026

1. The Cat in the Hat

U.S. release: November 6, 2026
Genre: Animation, comedy, family
Cast: Bill Hader, Matt Berry, Quinta Brunson, Xochitl Gomez

The mischievous Cat takes on a challenging assignment: cheering up siblings Gabby and Sebastian as they adjust to life in a new town. His magical adventure may be his last chance to prove himself.

2. The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping

U.S. release: November 20, 2026
Genre: Action, science fiction, drama
Cast: Joseph Zada, Mckenna Grace, Ralph Fiennes, Jesse Plemons, Elle Fanning

Set 24 years before the original story, the film follows a young Haymitch Abernathy as he enters the deadly arena, forms an unexpected alliance and joins an effort to challenge the Capitol.

3. Hexed

U.S. release: November 25, 2026
Genre: Animation, fantasy, coming-of-age
Cast: Hailee Steinfeld, Rashida Jones, Jodie Foster, Walton Goggins

Billie, an impulsive teenager, discovers hidden magical abilities. Alongside her cautious mother Alice, she enters the witch realm of Hexe and uncovers family secrets that could change its world.

4. Focker-In-Law

U.S. release: November 25, 2026
Genre: Comedy
Cast: Robert De Niro, Ben Stiller, Ariana Grande, Owen Wilson

A new chapter in the Meet the Parents franchise brings the Focker family back. The story focuses on the next generation as Henry prepares to marry Olivia.

Upcoming Hollywood movies in December 2026

1. Violent Night 2

U.S. release: December 4, 2026
Genre: Action, comedy, horror
Cast: David Harbour, Kristen Bell

The sequel returns to the holiday-action comedy world of a very unconventional Santa Claus. Full story details should be checked against the latest studio updates.

2. Behemoth!

U.S. release: December 4, 2026
Genre: Drama
Cast: Pedro Pascal, Olivia Wilde, Matthew Lillard

Tony Gilroy's drama follows a cellist who returns to Los Angeles and turns to composing music for films.

3. Zero A.D.: The Birth of Christ

U.S. release: December 11, 2026
Genre: Historical, religious drama
Cast: Sam Worthington, Ben Mendelsohn, Deva Cassel

A historical drama centred on the birth of Jesus Christ. Further plot details should be confirmed through official studio materials.

4. Avengers: Doomsday

U.S. release: December 18, 2026
Genre: Action, adventure, superhero, science fiction
Cast: Robert Downey Jr., Chris Evans, Chris Hemsworth, Pedro Pascal, Florence Pugh

Heroes from three different universes are set on a collision course as they face an existential threat unlike anything they have encountered before.

5. Dune: Part Three

U.S. release: December 18, 2026
Genre: Science fiction, adventure, drama
Cast: Timothée Chalamet, Zendaya, Jason Momoa, Florence Pugh, Robert Pattinson

Set nearly two decades after Paul Atreides took control of the Imperium, the story follows the consequences of his rule as new threats, betrayal and a far-reaching conspiracy emerge.

6. The Angry Birds Movie 3

U.S. release: December 23, 2026
Genre: Animation, comedy, family
Cast: Jason Sudeikis, Josh Gad, Emma Myers, Keke Palmer, Lily James

Red faces his biggest challenge yet: navigating fatherhood while helping to save the world. The flock returns for another family-friendly adventure.

7. Jumanji: Open World

U.S. release: December 25, 2026
Genre: Adventure, action, comedy, fantasy
Cast: Dwayne Johnson, Kevin Hart, Jack Black, Karen Gillan

A new Jumanji adventure is scheduled for Christmas Day. Detailed plot information remains limited, so check official updates as the release approaches.

Which movies should be on your watchlist?

For science-fiction fans, Avengers: Doomsday and Dune: Part Three are major December highlights. Families may prefer Hexed, The Cat in the Hat or The Angry Birds Movie 3, while comedy fans can look out for Focker-In-Law.

Final thoughts

The final two months of 2026 offer a broad mix of Hollywood entertainment. Keep this list handy, but always check local cinema listings: theatrical dates can differ by country and may change.

CewekPedia note

Dates shown are announced U.S. release dates. Theatrical dates in the UK, Canada, Australia and other markets may differ. Check local cinema listings; dates are subject to change.
